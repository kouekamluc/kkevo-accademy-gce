A car of mass 1200 kg accelerates uniformly from rest to 15 m/s in 5 s. What is the resultant force acting on it?

Correct — nicely done.
You found the acceleration first, then used F = ma.
1
Find the acceleration
a = (v − u) ÷ t = (15 − 0) ÷ 5 = 3 m/s²
2
Apply Newton’s second law
F = ma = 1200 × 3 = 3600 N
Why not 18 000 N? That multiplies mass by the final speed, which skips the time. Force depends on acceleration, not speed.
