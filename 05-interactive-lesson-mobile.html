What it shows

A resultant force makes an object accelerate. How much depends on force and mass:

Double F → double a
Double m → halve a

Worked example

A 60 kg pushcart gets a resultant force of 150 N. Find a.

a = F ÷ m
= 150 ÷ 60
= 2.5 m/s²
