# **Cameroon GCE Board Senior Secondary Curriculum Guide**

# **Comprehensive Program Syllabus for Form 5, Lower Sixth, and Upper Sixth (Arts & Science Streams)**

This document provides a complete synthesis of the Anglophone Secondary Subsystem curriculum in Cameroon, managed by the Ministry of Secondary Education (MINESEC) and assessed nationally by the Cameroon General Certificate of Education (GCE) Board. It covers the terminal year of the First Cycle (Form 5 / Ordinary Level) and the complete High School Second Cycle (Lower Sixth and Upper Sixth / Advanced Level) across both Science and Arts streams.

---

# **1\. Form 5 Curriculum (GCE Ordinary Level Exit Year)**

Form 5 represents the consolidation and national assessment phase of the First Cycle. Students complete rigorous subject syllabi based on the Competency-Based Approach (CBA / APC) to prepare for the GCE Ordinary Level examinations.

# **Science & Technical Stream**

## **Computer Science (Subject 0595\) & ICT (Subject 0596/0796)**

* **Web Design & Publishing:** HTML5 structural authoring (`<!DOCTYPE html>`, basic tag hierarchy, text formatting, hyperlinks, images, HTML tables, interactive forms). CSS styling principles (inline, internal, external CSS, selectors, typography, box model formatting).  
* **Legal, Ethical & Social Aspects:** Intellectual property rights, copyright, software piracy, open-source licensing (GPL, MIT, Creative Commons). Cameroon national legal frameworks governing cybersecurity, cybercrime, and data privacy regulated by ANTIC and MINPOSTEL. Socio-economic impacts including the digital divide, technological displacement, automation, e-waste, and remote collaboration.  
* **Examination Structure:**  
  * **Paper 1:** 1.5-hour Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) covering general theoretical breadth.  
  * **Paper 2:** Structured theory, logic circuit synthesis, pseudo-code writing, database schema design, and algorithm trace tables.  
  * **Paper 3 (ICT Practical):** Hands-on laboratory evaluation of word processing, spreadsheet modeling, RDBMS execution, and basic web design under timed conditions.

## **General Mathematics (Subject 570\)**

* **Set Theory & Logic:** Two- and three-set Venn diagrams, set algebra laws, logical deductions, and truth values.  
* **Commercial Mathematics & Variation:** Compound interest, depreciation, hire purchase, taxes, direct/inverse proportion, joint and partial variation.  
* **Quadratic Functions & Equations:** Factorization, completing the square, quadratic formula, graphical turning points, and roots.  
* **Sequences & Series:** Arithmetic Progressions (AP) and Geometric Progressions (GP)—nth term and sum formulas.  
* **Matrices & Vectors:** \$2\\times2\$ matrix operations (determinant, inverse, solving linear systems); 2D column vectors (magnitude, direction, collinearity).  
* **Advanced Trigonometry & Bearings:** Sine rule, cosine rule, non-right triangle area (\${\\frac{1}{2}}ab \\sin C\$), 3-figure bearings, and navigation.  
* **Circle Theorems & Statistics:** Cyclic quadrilaterals, alternate segment theorem; grouped frequency distributions, histograms, cumulative frequency ogives, median/quartile estimations, theoretical probability, and tree diagrams.  
* **Examination Structure:** Paper 1 (30% weight, 50 MCQs, 1.5 hrs); Paper 2 (70% weight, structured core and multi-part optional essay problems, 2.5 hrs).

## **Additional Mathematics (Subject 575 — Elective)**

* **Pure Mathematics:** Remainder and Factor theorems, partial fractions, radian measure, trigonometric identities, binomial expansions, coordinate geometry of lines and circles.  
* **Calculus & Mechanics/Statistics:** Differentiation from first principles, derivatives of \$x^n\$, tangents/normals, stationary points, definite integrals, area under curves; options in rectilinear kinematics and force resolution OR probability distributions.

# **Arts & Humanities Stream**

## **History (Subject 0560\)**

* **Trusteeship & Nationalist Movements in Cameroon (1945–1961):**  
  * *French Cameroun:* Trade unions, anti-colonial political parties (UPC, BDC, ESOCAM); radical nationalism under Ruben Um Nyobè, Felix Moumié, and Ernest Ouandié; the 1955 banning of UPC, armed insurrection (*the Maquis*), French counter-insurgency, internal autonomy under *Loi-Cadre* (1956), and independence on January 1, 1960, under Ahmadou Ahidjo.  
  * *British Southern Cameroons:* 1953 Eastern House of Assembly Crisis; regional political parties (KNC, KNDP, OK) led by E.M.L. Endeley, John Ngu Foncha, S.T. Muna, and A.N. Jua; the UN Plebiscite of February 11, 1961; Foumban Constitutional Conference (July 1961), and reunification on October 1, 1961\.  
* **Post-Independence Political Dynamics:**  
  * *Federalism to Unitary State:* The Federal Republic of Cameroon (1961–1972); centralism; single-party consolidation under the Cameroon National Union (CNU) in 1966; May 20, 1972 referendum, and creation of the United Republic of Cameroon.  
  * *Modern Constitutional Transitions:* Peaceful presidential transition in 1982 (Ahidjo to Paul Biya); 1984 state name change to Republic of Cameroon; 1990 "Liberty Laws", democratic revival, "Ghost Town" protests, Tripartite Conference, and the 1996 Constitutional Revision.  
* **World History since 1870:** Paths to African decolonization (peaceful transfers vs. armed liberation struggles); establishment of the OAU (1963) and AU (2002); Cold War dynamics (containment, Cuban Missile Crisis, proxy conflicts), collapse of the USSR, Arab-Israeli conflict, and the post-Cold War order.

# **2\. Second Cycle High School Curriculum (Lower Sixth & Upper Sixth)**

High School / Pre-University education (Second Cycle) is highly specialized, preparing students directly for university admission and professional certifications.

# **Science Stream Pathways**

## **Pathway A: GCE A-Level Computer Science (Subject 795\)**

### **Lower Sixth (Year 1\)**

* **Computer Organization & Architecture:** Von Neumann vs. Harvard models; Data, Address, and Control bus architecture; Fetch-Decode-Execute (FDE) cycle mechanics; CPU internal registers (PC, MAR, MDR, CIR, ACC); pipelining.  
* **Digital Logic & Combinational/Sequential Circuits:** Half/Full-Adders, Multiplexers, Decoders; Flip-Flops (SR, D, JK, T); Boolean algebraic simplification (De Morgan's Laws) and Karnaugh Maps (K-Maps up to 4 variables).  
* **Operating Systems & Translators:** Process management (FCFS, SJF, Round Robin); semaphores and deadlocks; Memory management (Paging, Segmentation, Virtual Memory, Page Faults); Compilers (Lexical, Syntax, Semantic analysis), Interpreters, Assemblers.  
* **Networks & Software Engineering:** OSI 7-Layer and TCP/IP models; IPv4/IPv6 addressing, CIDR subnetting algorithms; error detection (CRC polynomials); SDLC models (Waterfall, Agile/Scrum, Spiral); DFDs, ERDs, and UML modeling.  
* **Procedural Programming:** Structured execution in C or Python; subroutines, variable scoping, recursion, stack frames, file handling, and pointer management.

### **Upper Sixth (Year 2\)**

* **Abstract Data Types (ADTs) & Algorithms:** Stacks, Queues, Singly/Doubly Linked Lists, Binary Search Trees (BST insertion, deletion, traversals); Searching (Linear vs. Binary); Sorting algorithms (Bubble, Insertion, Selection, Merge, Quick Sort); Time/Space complexity analysis using Big-O notation (\$O(1)\$ to \$O(n^2)\$).  
* **Relational Database Design:** Formal Relational Algebra (\$\\sigma, \\pi, \\times, \\bowtie, \\cup, \\cap, \-\$); Functional Dependencies and Normalization theory (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF); Advanced SQL (DDL, DML, JOINs, nested subqueries).  
* **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP):** Abstraction, Encapsulation, Inheritance, Polymorphism; UML class diagrams, access modifiers, constructors/destructors; Exception handling in C++, Java, or OO-Python.  
* **Software Practicum:** Individual end-to-end software development project involving system design, coding, unit/integration testing, and formal technical reporting.

## **Pathway B: GCE A-Level Information and Communication Technologies (Subject 796\)**

### **Lower Sixth (Year 1\)**

* **Hardware Maintenance & Deployment:** Socket types, dual-channel RAM, BIOS/UEFI setup, POST diagnostics; OS deployment (Windows/Linux via USB/network), MBR vs. GPT partitions, file systems (NTFS, ext4), and virtual machines.  
* **Enterprise Infrastructure & Web Development:** E-commerce (B2B, B2C), VPNs, VoIP, LMS platforms; Green computing and e-waste management; Relational database automation; Semantic HTML5 layout, CSS3 Flexbox/Grid, and client-side JavaScript DOM manipulation.

### **Upper Sixth (Year 2\)**

* **Network Engineering & Cybersecurity:** RJ-45 termination (TIA/EIA 568A/B), VLANs, DHCP scope configuration; CIA Triad security model; Symmetric (AES) vs. Asymmetric (RSA) encryption, PKI, digital signatures, hashing (SHA-256); Firewalls, IDS/IPS, and Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP).  
* **Enterprise Productivity & Project Management:** Advanced Excel modeling (XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Pivot Tables, Solver, VBA macros); IT Project Management frameworks (Work Breakdown Structures, Critical Path Method, Gantt charts).

## **Pathway C: GCE A-Level Mathematics Framework**

Students select specific subject combinations tailored to their degree trajectory:

| Subject Code | Subject Name | Core Focus Areas | University Target Majors |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Subject 765** | Pure Mathematics with Mechanics (PMM) | Pure Mathematics \+ Classical Mechanics | Engineering, Physics, Computer Science, Architecture |
| **Subject 770** | Pure Mathematics with Statistics (PMS) | Pure Mathematics \+ Probability & Statistics | Economics, Actuarial Science, Medicine, Data Science |
| **Subject 775** | Further Mathematics | Advanced Pure, Advanced Mechanics & Numerical Methods | Honours Mathematics, Theoretical Physics, Engineering |

### **Core Pure Mathematics Syllabus (Compulsory for PMM / PMS):**

* **Algebra & Functions:** Mathematical induction, contradiction; partial fractions; Binomial theorem for rational indices; symmetric functions of roots (\$\\alpha+\\beta, \\alpha\\beta\$); composite/inverse functions, curve sketching with oblique asymptotes.  
* **Trigonometry & Coordinate Geometry:** Compound/double/half-angle identities; harmonic form \$R \\cos(\\theta \\pm \\alpha)\$; conic sections (circles, parabolas, ellipses, hyperbolas in cartesian/parametric forms).  
* **Differential & Integral Calculus:** Product, quotient, chain rules; implicit, logarithmic, and parametric differentiation; Maclaurin and Taylor series; Integration by parts, substitution, partial fractions; 3D volumes of revolution; 1st-order differential equations.  
* **3D Vectors:** Vectors in \$\\mathbb{R}^3\$, scalar (dot) products, vector equations of lines and planes, intersections, and distances.

# **Arts Stream Pathway**

## **GCE A-Level History (Subject 0760\)**

The Advanced Level History syllabus requires critical historiographical inquiry, primary document evaluation, and synthesis of competing historical arguments across three written examination papers.

### **Lower Sixth (Year 1\)**

* **Advanced Cameroon History (1884–1945):**  
  * *Legal & Border Historiography:* Textual critique of the Germano-Douala Treaty of July 12, 1884, and the Kings' Memorandum; Franco-German (1885, 1894, 1911\) and Anglo-German border conventions.  
  * *Comparative Imperialism:* In-depth comparative assessment of British Indirect Rule, French Direct Rule / Assimilation / Association, and German Imperial Policy.  
  * *Socio-Economic History:* Urbanization, forced labor regimes, mission education, early press, and socio-political associations (Jeucafra, Unikamerun).  
* **Modern World & European Revolutions (1848–1939):**  
  * *1848 Revolutions:* Socio-economic causes, ideological currents (liberalism, socialism, nationalism), course across France, Germany, and Austria, and reasons for failure.  
  * *European Unifications:* Diplomatic and military strategies of Cavour and Bismarck; Realpolitik; Italian and German unification impacts on European power balance.  
  * *Imperial Scramble & WWI:* Historiographical schools of thought (Eurocentric, Afrocentric, Peripheral, Marxist); causes and diplomacy of WWI; Paris Peace Settlement and structural weaknesses of the League Mandate system.  
  * *Interwar Crises:* Rise of Italian Fascism and German Nazism; collapse of the Weimar Republic; Anglo-French Appeasement policies.

### **Upper Sixth (Year 2\)**

* **Advanced Cameroon History (1945–Present):**  
  * *UN Trusteeship & Decolonization:* Evaluation of UN Visiting Mission reports and petitions; UPC insurrection, French counter-insurgency, pacification tactics, and political rise of Ahmadou Ahidjo.  
  * *Southern Cameroons Politics:* Party dynamics (KNC, KNDP, OK); constitutional conferences (Mamfe, Lancaster House, London); 1961 plebiscite mechanics; analytical evaluation of the Foumban Conference text.  
  * *Post-Colonial State Evolution:* Federalism vs. Unitarianism; single-party consolidation under CNU/CPDM; 1982 presidential transition; political liberalization in the 1990s, Tripartite Conference, 1996 Constitution, and modern governance.  
* **Advanced African & World History:**  
  * *Decolonization Typologies:* British constitutional transfers (Ghana, Nigeria); French *Loi-Cadre* and 1958 Referendum (Guinea's "No" vote); Armed liberation struggles (Algeria, Kenya Mau Mau, Portuguese colonies, Zimbabwean Chimurenga); Anti-Apartheid struggle in South Africa (1948–1994).  
  * *Cold War Geopolitics:* Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, containment, nuclear deterrence, superpower rivalries in Africa, Asia, and Latin America.  
  * *International Governance:* UN Charter, Security Council dynamics, peacekeeping operations (Congo, Balkans, Rwanda); Regional integration (OAU to AU, CEMAC, ECOWAS, SADC); Collapse of USSR (1991) and post-Cold War multipolarity.

# **4\. Complete Cameroon GCE Board Subject Inventory**

The Cameroon General Certificate of Education (GCE) Board administers national examinations across General (Ordinary Level & Advanced Level) and Technical/Vocational Subsystems. Below is the comprehensive classification of subjects offered across academic streams.

## **Science & Mathematics Stream**

* **Ordinary Level (Form 5 / Subject Codes 0500s–0700s):** General Mathematics (570), Additional Mathematics (575), Physics (0580), Chemistry (0515), Biology (0510), Human Biology (0535), Computer Science (0595), Information and Communication Technology (ICT \- 0596), Geology (0540), Food and Nutrition (0545).  
* **Advanced Level (Lower & Upper Sixth / Subject Codes 0700s):** Pure Mathematics with Mechanics (765), Pure Mathematics with Statistics (770), Further Mathematics (775), Physics (0780), Chemistry (0715), Biology (0710), Computer Science (795), Information and Communication Technology (ICT \- 796), Geology (0740), Food Science & Nutrition (0745).

## **Arts, Languages & Humanities Stream**

* **Ordinary Level (Form 5):** English Language (0530), Literature in English (0525), French (0545/0520), History (0560), Geography (0550), Religious Studies (0585), Logic (0555), Economics (0525/0510), Citizenship Education (0512).  
* **Advanced Level (Lower & Upper Sixth):** English Language (0730), Literature in English (0725), French (0720), History (0760), Geography (0750), Religious Studies (0785), Philosophy (0755), Economics (0725).

## **Commerce & Business Studies Stream**

* **Ordinary Level (Form 5):** Commerce (0520), Principles of Accounts (0505), Economics (0525), Office Practice (0572).  
* **Advanced Level (Lower & Upper Sixth):** Accounting (0705), Business Studies / Management (0712), Economics (0725).

## **Technical, Industrial & Vocational Tracks**

* **Technical O-Level & A-Level (ITC / ATC / CAP / BACC Technique):** Electrical Technology & Installation, Technical Drawing, Mechanical Engineering Craft Practice, Building Construction & Architectural Drafting, Woodwork / Carpentry, Surveying, Home Economics, Clothing & Textiles.
