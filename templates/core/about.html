Who it is for

Students preparing for the GCE

KKEVO Academy is made for students in Cameroon preparing for the GCE Ordinary Level and Advanced Level examinations, in the Arts and Science streams.

What we believe

Understand first, then practise

Memorising answers is not enough. Every topic is taught step by step, then practised, so that a wrong answer teaches you something instead of just marking you down.

Please read: what we are and are not

KKEVO Academy is an independent learning platform. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the Cameroon GCE Board. Our lessons follow the topics of the GCE syllabuses as closely as we can, but your teacher and the Board's own syllabus remain the final word on what is examined. Where textbooks or teachers differ, we say so in the lesson.
