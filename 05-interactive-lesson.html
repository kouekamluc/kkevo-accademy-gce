What the simulation shows

A resultant force makes an object accelerate, meaning its velocity changes every second. Two things decide how big that acceleration is:

More force → more acceleration
a is directly proportional to F. Double F, double a.
More mass → less acceleration
a is inversely proportional to m. Double m, halve a.

One newton is the force that gives a 1 kg mass an acceleration of 1 m/s².

Worked example

A pushcart of mass 60 kg at Mokolo market is pushed with a resultant force of 150 N. Find its acceleration.

1
Write the law: F = ma, so a = F ÷ m
2
Substitute: a = 150 ÷ 60
3
Answer with units: a = 2.5 m/s²
