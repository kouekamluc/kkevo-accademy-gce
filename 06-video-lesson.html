Understanding River Erosion

How a river wears away its bed and banks through four processes, and why rivers cut downwards in their upper course but sideways further downstream.

Key concepts

Hydraulic action
The force of moving water pushes into cracks in the banks and breaks rock apart.
Abrasion
Rocks carried by the river scrape and grind the bed and banks.
Attrition
Rocks in the load hit each other, becoming smaller and rounder.
Solution
Slightly acidic water dissolves rocks such as limestone.
